How to Play

Main Objective

Five cassette tapes are hidden somewhere in this maze. Each one holds a piece of a story that somebody didn't want told. Find all five to hear the whole thing.

You aren't alone in here. Something enormous lives in this maze, and it doesn't want that story found. It rules this place and everything that moves through it. Be careful. Trust nobody.

The Voice

You'll usually hear it before you see it. It listens for running, for heavy breathing, for doors. It notices light. Stay in one spot for too long and it starts to pick up your scent. If it comes after you, keep moving, and think twice about where you're leading it. And if it's been quiet for a while, don't assume it's gone. Listen above you.

Its Watchers

Smaller geese wander the halls. They can't hurt you on their own, but they'll follow you and call for their master. Shaking them off is your problem.

Temperature

Keep your body temperature in check. It climbs the longer you're in here, and faster when you're running or crammed into a hiding spot. The hotter you get, the weaker your body becomes: you slow down, you run out of breath sooner, and it gets harder to see straight. For the first 15 seconds of a run it doesn't rise at all.

Cool yourself down with anything cold you can find. Use your common sense. Let it get too high for too long and you'll collapse from heatstroke, and your run is over.

Flashlight

Your flashlight has limited battery and slowly recharges while it's switched off. It isn't only for seeing: aimed well, it's a weapon against certain creatures in the maze. Batteries are hidden around the maze, some out in the open and some tucked away where you'd have to look for them. Keep in mind that a light can be seen from far away.

Moving and Hiding

Sprinting is fast, loud, and burns through your stamina. Run dry and you'll be stuck catching your breath. Crouching is slow but almost silent. Some furniture will hide you, as long as nothing saw you climb in, and not every hiding place is only for hiding.

Items

Pick one item to bring into each run, and find more along the way. Cabinets, refrigerators and other spots can be searched, and what's inside can save your life. Every item does something different, and some are best saved for exactly the right moment.

Perks

Bring three perks into each run to bend it in your favor. They're self-explanatory: each one says exactly what it does on the Perks screen. During a run, a perk's icon in the top right lights up while it's working.

Playing Together

Open Multiplayer to make a room or join one with its code. The more of you there are, the harder the maze fights back, and the bigger it gets, with more tapes, refrigerators, batteries and cabinets to search. A tape found by anyone counts for everyone. Get caught and you can still watch your teammates until the run ends.

Playing as The Voice

Pick The Voice under Play to hunt AI survivors, or have the host of a room pick you. You wake up 10 seconds after the survivors do. Catch every one of them before they find all the tapes.

You see in the dark, in red, and you know every corridor. You don't see the survivors through walls, though. You hear them: running, breathing, slamming lockers and opening refrigerators show up on your screen as rings, roughly where the sound came from. You smell anyone who stays in one place too long, even inside a locker. Your surveillance geese honk when they spot someone, and survivors with their flashlights on light themselves up.

Bite (click) lunges where you're aiming and catches whoever you hit. Miss and you stumble for a second. Charge (hold Shift) is faster than any survivor, for 5 seconds. Honk (Q) shows you everyone nearby for a few seconds. Fly (F) takes you up over the walls for 14 seconds; bite while you're up there to drop on whoever's inside the red ring. Search (E) tears open the locker, bed or shower you're standing at. Taunt with 1 to 4.

The survivors fight back the same ways as always: a flashlight in your eyes blinds you, a blueberry muffin nearby is impossible to ignore, Iron Nerve punches you, and a pinned survivor can throw you off. Adam's Underwear makes them invisible to you.

Terms

Perks, items and other effects use a few words to keep things short:

Speed
How fast you move. "+20% Speed" means you move 20% faster.
Odorless
The Voice can't pick up your scent, however long you stay in one place.
Undetectable
Nothing can track you: not by sight, sound or smell.
Vitality
Your stamina doesn't run out.
Scotopia
You see everything clearly, as if every light in the maze were on, with no film grain, even with your flashlight off.
